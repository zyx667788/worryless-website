03About the company

Shenzhen-based.
US-focused.

Shenzhen Worryless International Logistics Co., Ltd. is an international freight forwarder specialising in China-to-USA shipping for importers, brands and e-commerce sellers.

We focus on two things and do them carefully: Air Freight DDP and Sea Freight DDP. In both, a single quote takes your goods from the supplier's door in China to their final destination in the United States — with export, import customs and duties already included.

Our name is our promise: we take care of the complex parts of cross-border logistics, so you can concentrate on selling.
