02How DDP works

Four steps from China factory to US door.

Under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) we act as your shipper of record end to end. You approve one quote — we take care of everything in between.

  1. STEP 01

    Share cargo details

    Tell us the product, weight or volume, pickup city and US delivery address. We reply with an all-in DDP quote.

    You
  2. STEP 02

    Pickup & consolidation

    We collect from your supplier or receive goods at our partner warehouse, then check, label and pack for export.

    Worryless · China
  3. STEP 03

    Export & main transport

    Export customs filing in China, followed by air freight or ocean freight to the United States.

    Air or sea
  4. STEP 04

    Cleared, paid, delivered

    US import clearance and duty payment, then final delivery to your door, 3PL or Amazon FBA center.

    Worryless · USA

One quote. One contact. No customs paperwork on your side.
